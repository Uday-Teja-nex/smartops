# Footage for the real demo

Record landscape 1080p at 25–30 fps, from a fixed phone on a tripod. Use a
wide view from 2–3 m high, angled down 20–40 degrees, with even lighting and
people visible head-to-foot. Avoid glare, backlighting, zooming and camera
movement. Record ten seconds of empty scene before and after each action.
Only stage scenes with consenting participants in a space you can use.
Label each file with client type, event and take number. Keep original files.

| Event | Office scene | Mall scene | Campus scene | Length/action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crowding | Reception lobby | Kiosk gathering | Cafeteria entrance | 60 s: 6–8 people enter marked area, stay 20 s, disperse |
| Queue | Reception desk | Checkout counter | Canteen counter | 90 s: 5 people queue for 30 s, then move through one at a time |
| Restricted entry | Marked meeting/server-room doorway | Staff-only doorway | Lab doorway | 45 s: one person crosses boundary, stays 10 s, leaves; repeat outside boundary as negative control |
| After-hours entry | Same doorway | Same doorway | Same doorway | Reuse restricted-entry clip with an explicit test schedule; capture time is not evidence of after-hours activity |
| Loitering | Entrance corner | Shop frontage | Bicycle-parking entrance | 120 s: one person stays 70 s, then leaves; second person walks past without stopping |
| Unattended bag | Lobby chair | Seating area | Bench | 150 s: person puts down backpack, walks more than 3 m away for 70 s, returns and removes it |
| Blocked exit | Staged marked doorway | Staged marked doorway | Staged marked doorway | 75 s: suitcase or person occupies test polygon for 30 s, then clears it; do not obstruct a real emergency route |
| Camera health | Any fixed scene | Any fixed scene | Any fixed scene | 60 s: normal view 15 s, cover lens 10 s, uncover 15 s, lower light 10 s, restore; disconnect/freeze tested separately at transport |

For every scene also record a 60-second negative control: normal movement,
no threshold crossed. Keep zone edges away from heavily occluded areas.
For bags, the camera must clearly show both bag and nearby floor; use a bag
large enough to occupy at least roughly 40 pixels at 960-pixel frame width.

Send footage files into sample-data/input plus a note confirming who recorded
them and permission for commercial demos. We also need a Telegram bot token
and destination chat ID for the notification gate, and optional prospect names
or logos. Supply credentials through local configuration, not committed files.
These inputs do not block implementation and transport tests.
